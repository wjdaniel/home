Welcome!
I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Earth and Environmental Science at Columbia University and Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory, advised by Dr. Terry Plank. My research integrates microanalysis, computational modeling and high-pressure high-temperature experiments to comprehend depth, timescales and variability of magmatic processes.
