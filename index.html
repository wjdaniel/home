Welcome!

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences at Columbia University and Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory, advised by Dr. Terry Plank.
My research integrates microanalyses, computational models and high-pressure high-temperature experiments to comprehend large scale subduction zone processes, individual volcanic eruptions and crystal scale processes. I am especially interested in understanding how volatiles are recycled at subduction zones and decoding unrest beneath active volcanoes.
My research allows me to work on samples all around the Pacific Ring of Fire from Alaska, Central America to Tonga.
